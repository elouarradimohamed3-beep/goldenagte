## Recommended speeds per screen

As a rule of thumb: about 3 Mbps for standard definition, 5 to 8 Mbps for HD, 10 to 15 Mbps for Full HD and around 25 Mbps for 4K. Multiply by the number of screens you plan to use at the same time.

## Your plan speed is not your real speed

Advertised speeds are maximums. Run a speed test on the device you watch on, at the time you usually watch. Evening hours are often slower than the middle of the day.

## Fixes for buffering

Use an Ethernet cable where you can, especially for the main TV. On Wi-Fi, move the router closer or use the 5 GHz band. Restart the router, close apps that use bandwidth, and avoid placing the router inside a cabinet.

## When it is not your connection

If other apps stream fine but one channel stutters, the cause may be that single feed. Note the channel genre and time, and message support so it can be checked.
