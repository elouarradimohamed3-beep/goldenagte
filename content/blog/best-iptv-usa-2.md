## What Is IPTV and How Does It Work?

IPTV (Internet Protocol Television) delivers television content over the internet instead of cable or satellite signals. All you need is a stable internet connection and a compatible device.

Unlike traditional TV, IPTV allows you to:

- Watch live TV
- Stream movies and TV shows on demand
- Replay past programs
- Watch on multiple devices

Content is streamed through secure servers and accessed using IPTV apps or players.
