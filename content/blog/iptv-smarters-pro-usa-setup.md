## What Is IPTV Smarters Pro?

IPTV Smarters Pro is a popular IPTV player application. It does not provide channels on its own. Instead, it acts as a media player that allows you to stream content from an IPTV subscription using login credentials or playlist details provided by your IPTV service.

In the USA, IPTV Smarters Pro is widely used because it supports live TV, video on demand (VOD), series libraries, electronic program guides (EPG), parental controls, and multi-device compatibility.

## Before You Start: What You Need

Before beginning your IPTV Smarters Pro USA setup, make sure you have the following:

## How to Install IPTV Smarters Pro in the USA

### Install on Android Devices

IPTV Smarters Pro is available directly from the Google Play Store in most regions.

1. Open the Google Play Store
2. Search for “IPTV Smarters Pro”
3. Install the app and launch it

### Install on Firestick or Fire TV

Amazon Firestick users need to sideload the app.

1. Enable “Apps from Unknown Sources” in Firestick settings
2. Install the Downloader app
3. Enter the official IPTV Smarters Pro download URL
4. Install and open the app
