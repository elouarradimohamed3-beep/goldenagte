## **What is IPTV?**

IPTV, or Internet Protocol Television, delivers television content over the internet rather than through traditional satellite or cable connections. Unlike conventional TV, IPTV streams content using internet protocols, giving you flexibility and control over what you watch.

There are three main types of IPTV services:

1. **Live TV Streaming:** Watch live channels in real time, just like cable TV.
2. **Video on Demand (VOD):** Access movies, shows, and series anytime you want.
3. **Catch-Up TV:** Replay programs you missed from the past few days.

IPTV is compatible with various devices including Smart TVs, Android boxes, Firestick, smartphones, and tablets. This makes it an ideal choice for modern homes with multiple devices.
