## Technical Deep Dive: Why Buffering Happens

Buffering is usually caused by:

- Overloaded shared servers
- Low bitrate encoding
- ISP throttling
- Insufficient home bandwidth

Advanced mitigation:

- Use wired connection
- Upgrade router firmware
- Use VPN only if ISP throttling confirmed
- Avoid overcrowded low-cost providers
