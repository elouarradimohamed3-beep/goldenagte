**Premium IPTV** is an IPTV service built for people who want the best picture, more screens at once and dependable support. It does not mean a different library of channels. It means the same large catalog delivered with higher quality, more simultaneous connections and a better experience when things go wrong.

This guide explains what premium IPTV includes, what you need to watch in 4K, how our premium plans compare and how to judge a premium claim. It is part of our guide to an [IPTV service](/) and connects to the [IPTV subscription](/iptv-subscription) and [IPTV USA](/iptv-usa) guides.

## What do you need to watch premium IPTV in 4K?

1. **A 4K TV or device** and a player that supports 4K.
2. **About 25 Mbps** of internet speed for each 4K screen.
3. **A stable connection.** Use Ethernet on the main TV when you can.
4. **A compatible player.** See the [installation guide](/install).

Learn more about the format in our guides to [IPTV 4K](/blog/iptv-4k) and [the best 4K IPTV services](/blog/best-4k-iptv-services).

## How to judge a premium IPTV claim

Premium is a word anyone can use. Check these points before you pay:

- **Trial or refund:** can you test it on your own devices?
- **Screens:** does the plan clearly state how many connections you get?
- **Quality:** is 4K described honestly as "where the source supports it"?
- **Support:** can you reach a person at any hour?
- **Price per month:** divide the total by the months.

## Who needs premium IPTV?

- **Families** where several people watch different content at once
- **Sports fans** who want the sharpest picture on a big screen
- **Shared homes** that need separate screens for each room
- **Small businesses** that need a few screens running together
