**IPTV USA** is simply live TV, movies and series delivered through your home internet connection in the United States. You replace the cable box with an app on a Smart TV, Fire TV Stick, Roku-type player, phone or computer, and you pay for a subscription instead of a cable contract.

This hub explains what you can watch, which devices work, how fast your internet needs to be, what it costs in US dollars and how to get started. It is part of our complete guide to an [IPTV service](/), and it connects to our [IPTV subscription](/iptv-subscription) and [premium IPTV](/iptv-premium) guides.

## What can you watch with IPTV in the USA?

A typical IPTV USA subscription includes live channels grouped by genre and an on-demand library:

- **Live sports:** football, basketball, baseball, soccer, combat sports and big events
- **News:** national, international and regional coverage
- **Movies and series:** a large on-demand catalog sorted by genre
- **Kids:** cartoons and learning shows
- **Documentaries and lifestyle:** nature, history, science and travel
- **Music and international:** music videos and channels from Canada, the UK and Europe

Coverage of specific local channels depends on your area, so ask before you buy if a particular broadcast matters to you.

## What internet speed do you need?

| Quality | Speed per screen |
| --- | --- |
| Standard definition | About 3 Mbps |
| HD | 5 to 8 Mbps |
| Full HD | 10 to 15 Mbps |
| 4K | About 25 Mbps |

Multiply by the number of screens watching at once. Run a speed test on the device you watch on and at the time you usually watch, because evenings are often slower. If you see stalls, read our fixes for [IPTV buffering in the USA](/blog/iptv-buffering-fix-usa) and what to do when [IPTV is not working](/blog/iptv-not-working-usa-fix).
