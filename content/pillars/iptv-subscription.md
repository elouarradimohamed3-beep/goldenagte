An **IPTV subscription** is the simplest way to watch live TV and on-demand movies and series without a cable box or a satellite dish. You pay once for the period you choose, receive a login within minutes and start watching in an app on the devices you already own.

This guide explains what an IPTV subscription includes, what it costs, how to compare plans and how to buy one safely. It is part of our wider guide to a reliable [IPTV service](/), and it links to our [IPTV USA](/iptv-usa) and [premium IPTV](/iptv-premium) guides.

## How much does an IPTV subscription cost?

IPTV subscription prices vary widely between providers. What matters is the price per screen per month, the length of the plan and what is included. When you compare offers, work out the monthly equivalent and check for hidden extras such as separate fees for the TV guide or for extra screens. Our own plans are all-inclusive. For a deeper breakdown, read [how much IPTV costs](/blog/how-much-does-iptv-cost) and our [IPTV subscription cost guide](/blog/iptv-subscription-cost-2025).

## Monthly or yearly: which IPTV subscription should you choose?

- **1 day ($7):** try the service on your own devices during a busy evening before you commit.
- **1 month ($20):** flexible if you only watch during a season or travel a lot.
- **3 to 6 months ($37 to $49):** a good middle ground once you know you like it.
- **1 year ($77):** the best value for most households.
- **2 years ($119):** the lowest monthly price if you are sure you will keep watching.

If you are undecided, start with a month or request a trial, then move to a yearly plan. See our comparison of [monthly versus annual IPTV subscriptions](/blog/monthly-iptv-subscription-vs-annual).

## How many screens do you need?

A standard plan covers one screen at a time. If several people watch at once, choose a plan with two to five connections. Premium plans for one year cover one to five screens at $77, $119, $149, $189 and $229. See the [premium IPTV guide](/iptv-premium) for what else a premium plan includes.

## How to buy an IPTV subscription safely

1. **Check the refund policy.** A clear refund window reduces your risk.
2. **Test support before you pay.** Send a question and see how quickly you get a helpful reply.
3. **Confirm your device works.** Look for a setup guide for your exact device, such as our [installation guide](/install).
4. **Compare monthly cost, not headline price.** A long plan can look expensive until you divide it by the months.
5. **Use a payment method you trust** and keep the receipt.
6. **Avoid pressure.** A good provider lets you take time to decide.

More advice is in our guide to [buying an IPTV subscription safely](/blog/buy-iptv-subscription-safely-2025).
